# Step 8.4 — Collective synthesis, final integration, independent verification and termination

**Owner approvals:** October 16, 2026 (core Step 8.4 and four later interface refinements: bounded integrator choices, separately protected project-specific synthesis/review budgets, version-aware five-member re-review, and sealed independent premortems). **Status:** STEP 8.4 CORE + FOUR INTERFACE POLICIES APPROVED; exact Step 3/4/5 DTOs, transaction guards, impact-propagation mechanics and failure-handling details remain IN DESIGN. **Runtime implementation:** NOT STARTED. **Runtime, security and comparative tests:** NOT RUN. No application implementation or tests authorized.

**Owner's defining correction:** Council should **not** ordinarily end by presenting five independently built alternatives and asking the user to pick the best one. The final collective discussion must identify **what each member did better and why**, reconcile compatibility/evidence, and convey those findings to a **dedicated final Integration/Synthesis LLM**. That execution agent produces **one integrated final deliverable** drawing on the strongest defensible contributions across the five investigations. **The original five members independently review the actual integrated deliverable again** before the existing premortem, report and user-acceptance boundaries. This same process must be **domain-agnostic**: software, research, scientific investigation, product design, engineering, reports and other user-approved deliverables.

This document supersedes any earlier *default final five-candidate winner-selection* interpretation, not the already accepted five-member independence, all-five ordinary checkpoint barriers, immutable local snapshots, sealed reviews, user authority, evidence graph, consent, or truthful readiness rules.

## 1. The distinguishing method: five independent investigations -> one traceable integrated result

Five equal Council members independently explore approaches under the user-approved Project Contract and semantic checkpoints. **Five fully production-complete applications or equivalent full deliverables are not mandatory** if the user-approved project objectives and review criteria allow useful prototypes, investigations, experiments, preliminary versions or evidence packages. For smaller projects, five complete alternatives may still be sensible. The Orchestrator's approved checkpoint plan (optionally informed by the development-pattern library) defines **reviewable outcomes**, not a forced solution strategy or arbitrary build duration. Members control their intellectual work and readiness; the runtime cannot fake progress.

After the mature/final investigation phase:
1. Record a real final investigation outcome for **all five seats**, pinned to exact artifacts/evidence; use `FINAL_CANDIDATE_READY` when a viable candidate is offered and `CONCLUDED_NO_VIABLE_CANDIDATE` for a genuinely concluded negative investigation. **BLOCKED, failed, disconnected, or budget-exhausted-without-conclusion does not count.** The final *submission* barrier is explicitly a distinct policy from ordinary implementation-ready checkpoint barriers and never claims all five produced working code.
2. Open a final **comparison checkpoint** using the existing trusted IRC-inspired gateway, customer-local immutable selected snapshots/authorized report artifacts, exactly five genuinely submitted **sealed independent reviews**, atomic reveal and structured evidence-linked peer discussions/verification-only `/work`. A member with no viable implementation **still participates as a full reviewer**. No one may write another member's authoritative candidate.
3. During final discussion identify **individually or jointly better components, results, techniques, interpretations, experiments, assumptions, verification methods, and limitations**. The goal is to synthesize what is *actually compatible and supported*, not award points by vote. Retain contextual failed approaches and legitimate disagreements.
4. Generate a **versioned Integration/Synthesis Blueprint** grounded in those independently attributed contributions. All five members can author, challenge and disposition blueprint propositions through the existing communication/action protocol; no additional intellectual judge and no requirement of unanimous agreement.
5. Complete the comparison checkpoint under its existing `/done`, pending obligation, closeout and verified grant/revocation rules. After closure, the trusted backend initializes a **new, separately authorized final-integration execution scope**; checkpoint access grants do **not** silently survive as Integration Agent permissions.
6. A **single logical Final Integration/Synthesis Agent** (a configurable permitted model, optionally using authorized subordinate tool/work tasks under its integration allocation) executes the blueprint in its own isolated customer-owned workspace/artifact area. It creates **one coherent final deliverable**, not a pile of five independent outputs. Its role is execution and attributable engineering/research synthesis, **not a sixth Council vote, trusted permission bypass, Orchestrator, final winner selector or hidden authoritative arbiter**.
7. Pin and mechanically verify the actual **integrated deliverable version**. The same **five original Council members independently review it again**, each submitting a sealed assessment before shared reveal/discussion and any approved verification-only checks. They compare the result against the Project Contract, blueprint promises, actual source/evidence, verified outcomes, omitted contributions, integration regressions and unresolved objections.
8. Required corrections produce **new integrated revisions** under the integration agent's own authority; revalidate/re-review affected evidence against the new exact version. Do not silently inherit passing results or reviewer approval from an old revision. Approved spend/resource ceilings still apply; incomplete or exhausted work remains explicitly incomplete rather than becoming a fake success.
9. Once an evidence-backed provisional integrated result and report version exist, conduct the existing **six-month-plus premortem** with all five members examining the same frozen actual integrated deliverable and provisional conclusions (not merely the earlier blueprint). Record plausible failure mechanisms as hypotheses, potential present-day checks versus future reassessment triggers, and any material changes/reviews necessary. No claim to have observed future facts.
10. Assemble an attributed final report and expose the **one integrated product/result** for the existing explicit **user final-candidate review**. User may accept, request revisions, reject, or cancel. Acceptance is distinct from observed customer-controlled durable export, verified reconstruction where applicable, cleanup and truthful `RunCompleted`.

## 2. Domain-general synthesis: reusable protocol, field-specific artifacts

**Software:** five approaches may contain runnable alternatives, prototypes, tests, or engineering evaluations. The blueprint names the baseline architecture selected for integration and the defensible features/algorithms/guardrails to adapt from each branch. The Final Integrator builds one runnable codebase with its own Git revision. Five members review the **new code**, reproducible execution evidence, security, regressions, compatibility and requirements. Direct copying is optional and conditional; incompatible components must be adapted or a design trade-off explained. Every claimed pass belongs to a real verified candidate revision.

**Scientific/academic research:** five independent literature searches, analyses, methodological proposals, numerical investigations or preliminary studies can reveal different evidence and weaknesses. The blueprint defines the supported questions, source claims, competing interpretations, admissible analysis methods, contradictory evidence and intended report structure. The Final Synthesizer authors **one evidence-attributed research report, synthesis, protocol or other approved research artifact**. It may perform newly authorized analysis and record new methods/results with correct provenance; **it may not invent studies, data, experimental results, citations, certainty or agreement**. Five original members review the actual integrated research output, its source lineage, reasoning, assumptions, coverage and uncertainties before final acceptance. A substantively inconclusive synthesis can be valid **only when the agreed research objective allows it**.

**Other fields:** hardware/robotics design -> one versioned integrated design and authorized prototypes/verification; business strategy -> one coherent plan with attributable evidence and competing assumptions; policy/legal/document work -> one traceable draft with scoped sources and unresolved conditions; analytical investigations -> one report and supporting reproducible analysis where required. **Git is not a global requirement for non-code artifacts.** Use the already approved content-hashed artifact store and versioned evidence ledger for PDFs/documents/datasets/designs as appropriate.

In every field, *best* means **best supported under the approved project requirements, context, tests/evidence and compatibility constraints**; not "each member must contribute a component", not one universal benchmark score, not a majority vote establishing truth, and not automatically the most recently produced answer.

## 3. Integration/Synthesis Blueprint as the handoff contract

One **canonical, immutable, versioned blueprint** binds **the Council's comparative intellectual direction as the integrator's default**, rather than handing the integrator a blank slate to re-adjudicate choices on the basis of its own model preferences. This is the owner-approved ADR-26 refinement of October 16, 2026; see §9.1 below.

One approved blueprint binds:
- project/run/approved contract revision and exact target deliverable(s) and acceptance conditions;
- current five-member final-submission set: member seat, outcome type, exact source/evidence manifest revision and limitations;
- referenced sealed/revealed peer review, canonical claim/source/experiment versions, concrete strengths and verified/contested observations;
- selected **contribution units** and intended role in the final product: baseline, directly reusable, adaptable, reimplement, analysis/method, excluded with recorded rationale, or unresolved;
- **compatibility and dependencies**: interface/contracts, data models, license/source permissions, conditions, environment, methods, evidence, trade-offs and known regressions/counterexamples; a superior component in isolation may be unsuitable in the combined system;
- an **embedded Council Decision Register** for each material architectural, methodological or interpretive choice: alternatives, the independent *pre-discussion* member positions when actually available, the five members' final *post-discussion* positions, any changes of mind and the cited reasons, observable supporting/contradicting evidence, shared-source correlations, which proposal the Council recommends under the Project Contract, and minority objections; no fabricated initial preference for a proposal first introduced during shared discussion;
- unresolved disagreements and alternatives with attribution; which uncertainties require verification, can be explicitly retained, or require *material user approval*;
- constraints on what the integration agent may modify, include, fetch, infer, rewrite, claim or transmit; completion/evidence and permissible artifact formats;
- version/content hashes, frozen authorized source set, relevant verdict/objection references, and any required customer authorization.

The gateway stores **complete original discussions and structured peer actions**; a trusted deterministic assembler indexes member-authored agreed/disputed contributions into the blueprint. Any language-model editorial summarization is **not an invisible intellectual decision**: preserve citations, authorship and unresolved choices. Members may propose a preferred foundational candidate or joint architecture. **The Council's evidence-backed recommendation is the default integration direction.** Count convergence as a meaningful, rebuttable signal *when evidence and requirements do not clearly discriminate*, especially if initial independent positions also converge. Do not treat votes as proof, imply statistically independent judgments from different model vendors without provenance, invent vote weights or lose minority-supported evidence. Evidence from one dissenter can outweigh four unsupported endorsements.

**No requirement that every member contributed accepted code or every proposal is combined.** A member may contribute a counterexample, warning, test method, alternative interpretation or a negative feasibility result instead. A blueprint that cannot reconcile a material incompatibility must label the conflict and authorized alternatives; the integrator cannot silently violate the Project Contract or pretend incompatible options were combined.

### Integration Agent decision authority

The Integration/Synthesis Agent **follows the Council's recommended high-level direction by default**, choosing ordinary mechanical implementation details *within* the approved synthesis constraints and recorded user priorities. It does **not** independently rerun the high-level vote/selection simply because its own training favors another method. A departure from a material Council recommendation needs an attributable, explicit evidence-, incompatibility- or approved-constraint-based **deviation record**, visible to all five on re-review; disagreements that cannot be safely resolved stay disclosed. Record ordinary choices against the exact Blueprint version and observed results. It may also discover newly incompatible assumptions, missing source or integration bugs. Those become **attributable deviation/change proposals** or observed failures. **Material scope/permission/spend changes require the existing explicit user approval**, and substantive departures from approved blueprint selections must be visible to subsequent five-member review—not buried in a prompt. It cannot overwrite the original five candidate histories or send raw peer files itself.

### October 16 owner-approved Step 8.5A handoff rules (ADR-30/31)

The five members record material integration recommendations, changes of position, counterevidence and unresolved objections **as authenticated structured member-authored decisions during final discussion**, not through a post-hoc model guessing what the conversation meant. The existing trusted gateway and canonical evidence ledger build the Decision Register/Blueprint from those authentic source records. It cannot invent five-member consensus, paper over absent member positions, manufacture citations, or suppress a minority objection; no unanimous technical vote is required.

The trusted backend validates and **freezes the exact versioned Blueprint** only with complete source/contract/review/position/objection provenance and the current authorized five-member discussion/obligation frontier. **Executable handoff is a later distinct observed state** requiring normal legitimate five-member `/done` and closeout, closed verification-only work, safely closed checkpoint and **independently verified snapshot grant/route revocation**. The Integrator then receives a *separate* user-approved source access grant, budget and workspace; a frozen hash, a request to close or a forced-incomplete closeout alone do not authorize execution. An integrator's private workspace is not writable by the original Council members during review.

## 5. New final deliverable verification and iteration

The integrated output is **not validated by tests run only on the five precursor candidates**. The trusted runtime records the precise integrated revision/artifact content hash, implementation environment where relevant, the verification definition and observed outcomes from protected or member-requested disposable verification. Before the user sees a claim of "ready", the applicable verification/readiness requirements are recorded as PASSED/FAILED/BLOCKED/NOT_RUN, with real evidence; member assertions cannot mark a protected test PASSED.

**Review round:** exactly the *same five fixed logical members*, independently assessing the *same frozen integrated version*. Their assessments remain sealed until five genuine submissions exist; missing/exhausted/disconnected members remain missing and cannot be replaced with the integrator's opinion. The subsequent revealed discussion may propose changes; `/work` remains **verification-only**, not integration edits. Revisions are performed by the Final Integrator *outside review*, in a newly authorized integration implementation interval after proper closure/revocation. The changed output receives new content hashes and relevant new review/test versions. If prior checks no longer apply, show them as **STALE / REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED**, not PASSED. Define affected-review reuse with actual evidence rather than assumed equivalence; default conservative re-review for material changes.

**Revision loops:** repeats are allowed under the **existing approved budget, resource, per-operation retry and user decision rules**. There is no automatically decreed "maximum intellectual improvements" and no infinite unconditional approval of spending. Exhaustion or required missing evidence yields an honest `INCOMPLETE` or user-authorized continuation gate. Existing event-clock/idempotency/outbox/UNKNOWN reconciliation remains authoritative across all integration tool, export, Git, provider and artifact effects.

## 6. Existing six-month-plus premortem, now targeting the integrated result

After a provisional integrated artifact and report version are available and reviewed as required, the five original members each independently examine the *same frozen integrated result and report* under the existing hypothetical premise: a careful investigation at least six months later finds material error/incompleteness. Each records **affected integrated artifact/claim revisions, assumptions, causal mechanism, present-day testable checks, future-dependent observations and confidence limitations**. They do not claim to have witnessed future events. **Approved:** the premortem is a distinct independent SEALED review round. All five authentic original-member hypothetical failure analyses target the same pinned integrated deliverable and provisional report revisions and remain reviewer-private until five submissions exist and the trusted runtime atomically reveals them. No new intellectual judge or sixth reviewer is authorized.

Material premortem findings can reopen **affected integration changes/verification/review** only under the already approved budget, scope and user gates; untestable risks remain clearly labeled limitations/reassessment triggers. A materially revised integrated artifact invalidates version-dependent premortem statements until reviewed against the correct version. Do not claim all concerns were resolved merely because a report was drafted.

## 7. Final report, acceptance and teardown: one product, honest status

A drafting/editorial model may assemble the user report from **existing versioned, attributable** evidence and observed verification. It cannot invent empirical research, promote unverified analysis to proven, hide minority concerns, silently choose an unreviewed alternative or overwrite five original positions. The user receives:
- the **one integrated final deliverable** (or an honestly inconclusive/blocked outcome when no valid unified deliverable exists under the agreed scope);
- exactly what was reused/adapted from each original investigation and **why**, including incompatible/excluded parts and preserved negative results;
- final version identities, actual verification/clean-rebuild/environment evidence where applicable, all five review dispositions, relevant unresolved disagreements and the premortem;
- remaining requirements/coverage gaps, costs/budgets, source/privacy/license limitations and original candidate evidence references.

**User acceptance remains explicit** via existing `FINAL_CANDIDATE_REVIEW` and `AcceptCandidate`/`RequestCandidateRevisions`/`RejectCandidate`/`CancelFinalization` command contracts, now targeting the **pinned integrated deliverable** by default. No model or majority automatically accepts the result. Research may validly conclude inconclusively if that is within the approved research objective. A software-delivery contract is **not** fulfilled by a mere negative research report without explicit user-approved scope change.

Accepted does not imply delivered: archive only authorized source/artifacts to the customer's selected storage, verify required hashes/reconstruction and references, reconcile unresolved consequential operations, and observe cleanup/credential revocation before claiming COMPLETED. Retain original candidates/lessons **only** under customer retention policy; no vendor-controlled global sharing by default.

### 9.1 ADR-26 — Bounded intellectual discretion for the Final Integrator

The five original members own investigation and comparative evidence/objections. The versioned Blueprint is the binding handoff of findings, constraints, trade-offs and source/evidence references. It is not a list of five mandatory code fragments to paste; techniques that work separately can be mutually incompatible.

**Within the existing user-approved Project Contract and synthesis scope,** the Integrator may resolve a real architectural/methodological ambiguity, choose an implementation baseline or integration technique, and make ordinary technical/design decisions. It is an authorized **execution decision**, not a Council vote, a finding automatically proven true, or permission to change approved user requirements. It must write an attributed **Integration Decision Record** referencing:
- its exact Blueprint revision, contested contribution options/member positions and underlying evidence/compatibility conditions;
- whether the choice used stronger applicable verification, a contract priority/trade-off, or an explicitly labeled *provisional/reversible* judgment where evidence is insufficient;
- the chosen method, known alternative(s), expected impact/limitations, proof or absence of proof, and required subsequent verification;
- exact integration output revision(s) caused, exposed blueprint deviation, and the originating operation/attempt IDs.

**Owner-approved ADR-26 refinement: Council intellectual direction comes first; support is a rebuttable signal, never proof.**

**Council Decision Register (inside the existing Blueprint; no new service):** each *material* disputed or selected issue records a choice/issue ID, versioned alternatives, scope/compatibility, original member seats, any authentic **sealed pre-discussion positions**, **final post-discussion positions**, why a seat changed its position, actual evidence and source independence/overlap, the Council-recommended direction if supported, whether the finding is `RECOMMENDED`, `CONTESTED`, `UNRESOLVED` or `REQUIRES_USER_DECISION`, and all dissenting supported counterexamples. No absent initial position is invented; a choice first proposed during shared discussion simply lacks pre-discussion stances.

**How preference affects integration:** when several candidates survive hard constraints, Council convergence and recommendation get **more weight as a default**—particularly if several independent pre-discussion positions converge on the same evidence-supported recommendation. However, numeric headcounts are not calibrated probabilities or automatic truth. Multiple members may share the same training/source error or have been persuaded during discussion; record common citations and distinguishing reasons. **A demonstrated single-member counterexample can override a four-member preference.** Do not hard-code a generic 4/5 threshold, mathematical weighting scheme, veto vote, or universal preference ranking. Evidence, allowed conditions and the approved user objective take priority; convergence is a secondary but meaningful tie-break / preference signal.

**Decision order for actual Integrator execution:**
1. **Hard requirements, permissions, IP/egress, protected verification and demonstrated counterevidence are constraints.** No model majority and no Integrator belief overrides them; affected inadmissible operations are blocked.
2. **Use the Council's recorded evidence-backed recommended direction as the default.** The Integrator is a bounded executor of this collective technical/research judgment, not a new selector of five peer approaches. Do not silently substitute another framework, method, analysis or source selection because the Integrator prefers it.
3. **When relevant evidence is tied or incomplete:** give genuine independent Council convergence an appropriately *greater qualitative influence*. Preserve the minority case, source correlations, limitations and evidence gaps; where testable, request an authorized targeted investigation/verification instead of reflexively voting again.
4. **If the question stays unresolved but is reversible and within the approved contract:** the Integrator may choose a documented provisional path, **prefer the Council recommendation when one exists**, state relevant uncertainties and define an appropriate later verification obligation. It cannot mark the preference proven.
5. **A material departure from a Council recommendation** requires an **Integration Deviation Record** linking the specific Council Decision Register entry, conflicting verified evidence or newly discovered incompatibility, discarded alternatives, relevant consent/constraints and exact revised output. The change is expressly surfaced to all five original members during post-integration sealed re-review. Mere Integrator stylistic preference, confidence or different pretraining is insufficient.
6. **Irreversible, safety-critical, materially unauthorized or contract-changing disputes** require existing escalation/user authorization through the faithful Orchestrator; dependent implementation remains blocked when needed. Neither model nor majority may invent the user's priorities. Budget exhaustion does not authorize evasion.

**Intended weight:** Council members decide *what is defensibly preferred and why*; the Integrator decides ordinary *how to assemble it*. The Integrator can question a finding using new evidence and cannot be forced to knowingly implement a demonstrable defect, but it must neither hide dissent nor substitute its own high-level agenda. All five genuine members then independently review the exact actual integrated output again.

The integration agent has **no peer vote, no authority to rewrite a member's historical reasoning, no default right to directly send files or mount peer workspaces**, and is not a new supervisor. Its operational decisions are always subsequently reviewable by the fixed five peers.

### 9.2 ADR-27 — Five separately protected and user-approved budget scopes

Council's setup/Project Contract and Step 7 budget records must propose and obtain **project-specific token/cost/resource ceilings** for the following **distinct chargeability scopes** (no fixed default percentages):
1. **Independent member work:** all five member workspaces and their delegated workers.
2. **Checkpoint peer comparison:** sealed assessments, IRC gateway discussion, ordinary checkpoint `/work` and synthesis-blueprint examination.
3. **Final Integration/Synthesis execution:** dedicated integrator model calls, workspace operations, tool work, revision attempts and its internal workers.
4. **Post-integration validation and premortem:** five independent integrated-product reviews, re-review allowances, supported verification, five hypothetical failure reviews and required follow-up.
5. **Orchestration/final report/archival overhead:** approved contract/clarifications, report drafting, accepted-deliverable export and operational execution where chargeable.

**No prior 70/25/5 or illustrative 45/15/20/15/5 split becomes the universal default.** A reasonable candidate distribution and uncertainty range can be displayed based on project type, evidence and selected providers, but **only the user's versioned approval makes a spend cap authoritative**. The same principle applies to research synthesis and non-software deliverables; costs need not match software build ratios.

**Funding gates:**
- Before admitting the Final Integrator, reserve/admit against its **own protected integration scope**, and check that a user-approved **post-integration review/premortem scope exists with sufficient allowance for the minimum authorized next required operations**; a mere estimated sufficiency is not a promise to finish.
- The operation processor transactionally checks **total run + relevant member/parent/worker + phase + integration/review scope**, explicit currency/token ceilings and conservative unknown-provider/pricing liability **before each dispatch** as Step 5/7 already require.
- One call debits **exactly one chargeability scope** while concurrently enforcing all higher-level ceilings; avoid double counting and concurrent overcommit.
- The Integrator cannot spend the five reviewers' protected allowance, and review agents cannot consume the integrator's remaining balance by convention. Redistribution/top-up is an **explicit user-approved immutable budget policy revision**, not a smart suggestion silently applied.
- Exhaustion suspends only affected new admissions with preserved progress and actual pending/UNKNOWN liabilities; it **never** becomes a passing integrated-review certificate, automatic budget transfer or implicit project acceptance.
- On resumption, recheck model access, data egress, contract, approved policy revision, host resources, idempotent pending effects and current source versions.

Any sample numerical allocation is **illustrative, not approved policy or an empirical forecast**. The original editable 70/25/5 planning suggestion is historical for this new mandatory integration architecture, not the setup default.

### 9.3 ADR-28 — Version-aware review invalidation with five genuine reviewers

The initial integrated deliverable always receives **five real sealed independent assessments** against the **same exact pinned content/manifest version** before reveal. This remains mandatory for software, research, engineering, documents and other domains.

Any later Integrator revision produces a **new immutable integrated-result version**, with a content hash, exact changed artifact/evidence/claim references, a separate authorized review generation where substantive change is present, and explicit prior-review applicability status. The impact calculation follows the Step 6 typed evidence/dependency graph, file/manifest dependencies and approved tests, **but an absent edge is not proof of no dependency**.

Three categories govern the next action:

| Change classification | Required effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Demonstrably non-substantive/equivalent** (e.g. pure formatting with defensible verification of relevant semantic equivalence) | New version and documented equivalence/impact evidence, without inheriting unsupported verification. Previously valid claim-specific dispositions may remain *applicable to unchanged conditions*; user-visible final artifact version must still be exact. If evidence cannot prove equivalence, escalate classification. |
| **Localized substantive change with defensible dependency closure** | Fresh **five-member independent SEALED delta assessments** of the same new pinned version, changed units, relevant dependencies, updated required verification and previously disputed obligations. Each of five actually submits anew, even if it accepts some attributable *unchanged* prior conclusions. Scoped review is not a single reviewer certifying all five. |
| **Foundational/widespread/high-risk/uncertain impact** | **Full fresh five-member sealed review** and applicable re-execution/revalidation of required checks against the new pinned version; no ungrounded applicability inheritance. |

When an impact classifier cannot determine whether a modification affects prior conclusions, choose the **broader review**; security-sensitive changes, broad data model/assumption shifts and altered acceptance criteria cannot automatically be treated as local.

**Safe state transitions:** the integrator only modifies its integration workspace **outside** active read-only peer reviews, after trusted checkpoint closure, fencing and access revocation. The trusted runtime records each review scope/generation, source/artifact hash, five distinct reviewer-seat submissions, independent private assessments and atomic reveal. A late submission for an older revision is rejected as stale for the new round but retained as historical evidence. A prior PASS at a previous revision remains an accurately scoped **historical result**; affected claims/checks become `STALE / REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED` until actually rerun or explicitly evidenced as applicable. A bad new implementation cannot inherit an old passing build, study verification or assessment.

**Failure/recovery:** any member missing, blocked or exhausted during required five-peer re-review leaves that round INCOMPLETE rather than fabricating five verdicts. Re-run model calls and re-review consume the approved post-integration validation allowance; no unconstrained loop or forced consensus. The user may authorize more resources, revision, or a truthful partial/inconclusive stop via existing user controls.

### 9.4 ADR-29 — Independently sealed six-month-plus premortems

After an independently reviewed **frozen integrated artifact version** and **frozen provisional report version** exist, the runtime opens a **distinct, version-bound SEALED_PREMORTEM round** for the **same five original Council members**, not the Integrator.

Each reviewer sees the exact same authorized integrated deliverable/report versions and, **without access to other members' premortem text**, produces a hypothetical account of plausible failures discovered at least six months later. Required minimum fields: reviewer seat, target artifact/report hashes, affected claim/assumption versions, plausible mechanism and evidential status, what can be checked now, what requires later observation, potential impact and limitations. Hypothetical future scenarios are never recorded as observed empirical evidence.

**Atomic reveal:** all five genuine current-round submissions must exist and refer to the same frozen version pair before the existing trusted sealed-review barrier can reveal them. Missing or unreachable reviewers, unapproved spend or invalid target versions produce an explicitly incomplete round; no fake fifth assessment, automatic agree/disagree result or substituted integrator review.

After all-five reveal, existing peer action/challenge and **verification-only `/work`** protocols address testable concerns. Material findings that require modifying the deliverable must go through the separate integrator revision path and **invalidate affected review/premortem claims according to their actual version dependencies**. The relevant affected premortem must be reconsidered against the new artifact/report version, while intact historical premortem scenarios remain attributable evidence. If dependency impact cannot safely be scoped, rerun the full five-member premortem against the new frozen version pair. Untestable future contingencies remain explicitly labeled limitation/reassessment triggers. There is no promised automatic wake-up after six months.

### 9.5 Integration interfaces to reconcile (still design, not implemented)

Use **existing** Unified Trust command processor, PostgreSQL canonical event clock/outbox, trusted snapshot provider, existing members and reviewers, Step 6 graph, Step 7 budget system and current UI. No new database, custom prompt-based moderation judge, per-worker lifecycle service or new voting seat.

**Step 3 tables/records to refine:** blueprint choice/deviation/version refs; applicable contract priorities; budget policy version and five separate protected allowance accounts; integrator decision/event/attempt refs; artifact-to-dependency impact manifests and equivalence evidence; review generation/scopes and five independent reviewer submissions; sealed premortem round (target artifact + report versions) and five genuine scenario submissions; disposition/invalidation and audience/retention controls.
