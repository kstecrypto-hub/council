## October 16 — ADR-26–29 approved Step 8.4 interface refinements

**ADR-26 — Bounded Integrator discretion.** The non-voting Final Integrator may make evidence-grounded technical or methodological decisions within the approved Blueprint/Project Contract and priorities; records alternatives, compatibility, limitations, source citations, provisional choices, exact produced revisions and any substantive deviation. Majority votes do not establish truth. New requirements, scope, egress, permissions or spending require explicit user approval.

**ADR-26 owner-approved refinement — Council recommendation governs direction:** The five independent Council members are the primary intellectual decision-makers for **which** architectural/research/engineering direction is favored and why. Convergence gets **qualitative extra weight** when evidence/requirements do not discriminate, but neither 4/5 nor 5/5 is proof; a single verified counterexample can outweigh a majority. **The Integrator follows the Council recommendation by default**, owns bounded implementation/how decisions, and may materially depart only with a traceable evidence-/compatibility-based deviation record for original five-member re-review. Not an invented weighted voting formula, sixth reviewer, automatic veto or a fresh Integrator high-level election. The user controls material scope/permissions/budget/acceptance changes.
